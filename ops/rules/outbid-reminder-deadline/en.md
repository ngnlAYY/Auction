# Outbid Reminder Rules

Marking equipment on the website gives you an outbid reminder. The auction database remains the authority.

- Choose one reminder channel in “My”: MoogleMail or a forum private message. A single outbid event produces one reminder, on the channel you selected.
- A reminder is sent when you were leading on that equipment and someone else takes the lead. Bidding on your own item does not start a new leading period.
- You may receive one reminder per leading period. Reminders are never merged and are never capped by a daily or per-auction limit.
- A reminder only counts until that equipment's effective deadline, including any soft-close extension. After the deadline, reminders that have not started sending are cancelled and are not sent later.
- This applies to both channels: MoogleMail reminders are recorded as an unsent preflight failure, and forum private messages are retired from their queue. No other mail is affected.
- A reminder that already started sending when the deadline passed is not resent. If its result is unknown, an operator reconciles it manually.
- Near the deadline, rely on the website or a proxy bid rather than waiting for a reminder.
